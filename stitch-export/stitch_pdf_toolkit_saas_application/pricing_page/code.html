Frequently Asked Questions

Have more questions? Check out our help center or reach out to our team at support@pdftoolkit.com

Can I cancel my subscription?

Yes, you can cancel your Pro or Business subscription at any time from your settings page. You will retain access until the end of your billing cycle.

What file formats are supported?

We support PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, JPG, and PNG. You can convert between these formats seamlessly using our toolkit.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. All files are encrypted using AES-256 and are automatically deleted from our servers 2 hours after processing.

Do you offer educational discounts?

Yes! Students and educators with a valid .edu email address are eligible for a 50% discount on the Pro plan.
